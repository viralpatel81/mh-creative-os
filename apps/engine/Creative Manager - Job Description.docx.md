# Creative Manager

## Overview

At TierPoint, we are technologists, trendsetters, explorers, and creative minds who push the limits of imagination. We are seeking someone who thrives in a fast-paced, collaborative environment and brings structure, accountability, and efficiency to how creative work gets done.

The **Creative Manager** is responsible for leading the execution and operations of TierPoint’s creative function. This is a **project management–first role** focused on owning the end-to-end creative workflow—from intake and briefing through execution, review, and delivery—while ensuring high-quality output across all deliverables.

This role will also support copy development through writing, editing, refinement, and process oversight, including building scalable systems that leverage AI to enable efficient copy creation and retrieval. This role will also develop original copy for campaigns, events, and marketing materials. 

Reporting into Brand & Creative, this role serves as the **central hub for all creative requests**, partnering primarily with Marketing while also supporting cross-functional teams such as HR, Site Operations, and others as needed.

Approximately **70–75% of this role is focused on creative operations, workflow management, and project leadership**, with **25–30% dedicated to copy** editing, proofreading, and messaging refinement**copy editing, refinement, and light writing support**.

---

## What You’ll Do (Core Competencies)

### Creative Operations & Project Management Leadership

* Own and manage the **end-to-end creative pipeline**, including intake, prioritization, briefing, kickoff, timelines, and delivery across all marketing and cross-functional projects.

* Serve as the **primary project manager for all creative work**, ensuring visibility, accountability, and on-time execution.

* Lead and standardize the **creative briefing process**, ensuring clear inputs, objectives, and deliverables.

* Facilitate **project kickoff calls and ongoing stakeholder alignment** to ensure clarity and momentum.

* Manage capacity, timelines, and resource allocation across internal designers and external partners, proactively flagging risks or gaps to ensure timelines stay on track.

* Identify and implement process improvements to increase **efficiency, predictability, and throughput** of creative work.

### Copy Workflow, QA & Scaled Enablement

* Design and implement a **scalable system for copy creation, retrieval, and reuse**, ensuring alignment to approved messaging and brand standards.

* Enable internal teams to **self-serve copy responsibly**, supported by clear guidelines, templates, and structured governance.

* Serve as a **quality gate for all copy**, leading rigorous review and approval workflows to ensure clarity, accuracy, consistency, and effectiveness before publication.

* Apply strong **copywriting and editing expertise** to refine messaging and elevate overall content quality across deliverables.

* Leverage tools, including AI, as **supporting enablers** to increase efficiency and scale—while maintaining human oversight and editorial judgment as the standard.

* Continuously optimize workflows to improve speed, quality, and consistency across all outputs.

### Creative Execution & Copy Support

* Edit and refine copy across marketing channels, including web, email, paid media, social, sales enablement, and campaigns.

* Provide **light copywriting support** for priority initiatives when needed.

* Translate technical or complex inputs into clear, structured content in collaboration with subject matter experts.

* Ensure all deliverables meet **quality standards, clarity expectations, and project requirements** prior to release.

### Cross-Functional Collaboration

* Partner with Marketing teams (Growth, Product Marketing, SEO) to ensure creative aligns with campaign goals and inputs.

* Support cross-functional teams (HR, Operations, etc.) with structured creative intake and execution.

* Work closely with external agencies, freelancers, and contributors to **coordinate work, provide input, and ensure alignment with timelines, scope, and quality expectations**, in partnership with Brand & Creative leadership.

## What You Bring (Requirements)

* **5–8+ years of experience** in creative operations, project management, marketing, or a related discipline.

* **5–8 years of hands-on copywriting experience**, with strong editorial judgment and core copy skills including messaging clarity, tone consistency, grammar, storytelling, and audience alignment.

* Proven expertise in **managing complex creative workflows and multiple concurrent projects**.

* Strong background in **project management, process design, and operational efficiency** within a creative or marketing environment.

* Experience editing and refining copy across digital and campaign-based channels.

* Familiarity with **AI tools for content creation and workflow optimization**.

* Ability to work with and operationalize **messaging frameworks developed by Product Marketing and Brand teams**.

* Strong ability to interpret technical or complex subject matter and translate it into clear, structured outputs.

* Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.

* Experience **collaborating with agencies, freelancers, and cross-functional contributors** within a structured creative process.

### Tools & Systems Experience

* Experience with **project and workflow management tools** such as Monday.com, Smartsheet, Basecamp, Jira, Asana, or Workfront.

* Familiarity with **collaboration and content tools** such as Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Workspace, or SharePoint.

* Experience with **creative and asset management tools** such as Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, or Digital Asset Management (DAM) systems.

* Exposure to **AI-powered content tools** (e.g., ChatGPT, Jasper, Writer, or similar platforms) and the ability to apply them within structured workflows.

### Industry Experience

* **B2B marketing experience is required.**

* Experience in **data center, cloud, infrastructure, professional services, or managed services environments strongly preferred.**

## Success Profile (What Good Looks Like)

* Creative work moves **efficiently, predictably, and on time** with clear ownership and minimal bottlenecks.

* Stakeholders have a **structured, reliable process** for submitting and executing creative requests.

* Teams can **generate and access copy efficiently using AI**, within defined guardrails.

* Creative output is **consistent, high-quality, and aligned to provided messaging inputs**.

* The creative function operates as a **disciplined, scalable system**, not a reactive service model.
