## Style

Indiana farm meets premium pet aisle. Warm cream grounds with deep ink type, forest and pine greens for trust, and an earthy ochre for energy. Nothing glossy, nothing synthetic. The feel of a butcher's paper wrap and a feed-sack stencil — handmade authority, not hipster nostalgia. Confident, never cute.

## Composition

Generous margins. Type does the work — big extrabold sans-serif headlines, lowercase customer quotes treated as pull-quotes, one strong product or farm photo per frame. Hierarchy is dominant headline → short proof line → CTA. Photography sits in a tall vertical block or fills the frame edge-to-edge; no rounded card chrome.

## Texture

Subtle paper grain across the background. Faint kraft-paper warmth in shadows. Photo treatment is natural light, slightly warm white balance, no filter — like the photo was taken on the farm at 4pm.

## Motif

Single-ingredient close-ups (a slice of beef heart, a scoop of goat-milk powder), barn-board and Indiana landscape, freeze-dryer chamber details, hands of the family at work. Repeating visual phrase: one ingredient + one dog + one farm context.

## Image Style

Documentary food and farm photography. High dynamic range natural light. Real dogs, real bowls, real hands. Color graded toward warmth — cream highlights, forest mid-tones, deep umber shadows. Never overlit, never plated for Instagram.

## Avoid

Stock pet imagery. Cartoon dogs or paw-print iconography. Bright cyan, magenta, neon greens. Glossy product renders. Vector illustration with thin uniform strokes. Corporate teal gradients. Lifestyle photography with influencer staging. Comic Sans of any kind. Filtered Insta-warmth that hides the food.

## Image Prompt

A documentary still-life on a Indiana family farm. Warm cream background (paper or unbleached linen). [SUBJECT] rendered as a real, tangible scene — single-ingredient freeze-dried product, real dog at home, or hands at the freeze-dry line. Natural late-afternoon light from one side, soft shadows, no fill. Color palette is cream ground, deep near-black ink, forest green and pine green accents, one earthy ochre highlight. Texture: faint paper grain, real surface, dust motes acceptable. Photography style: large-format film food editorial, low saturation, generous negative space, type-friendly composition with the subject offset to one side. No CGI, no overlay graphics, no text, no logos, no rounded UI chrome. 1:1 square unless otherwise specified.

## Content Types

### hero-stat
Single huge number (e.g. `96%`) in extrabold Nunito Sans over cream ground, with a one-line caption in forest green underneath.

### comparison
Two-column "Freeze-Dried vs Kibble" board. Forest green check column on the left, charcoal X column on the right. Type-driven, no icons.

### quote-card
Customer review in lowercase serif italic on cream, with the dog's name pulled out in ochre uppercase eyebrow above. Verified-customer attribution in small caps.

### bts-photo
Edge-to-edge photo of the farm, family, or process. Single one-line caption in the lower-left over a paper-grain bar.

### product-card
Product photo on cream, name in extrabold sans, recipe (e.g. "Beef Recipe") in ochre uppercase eyebrow, single proof line, Shop Now CTA in deep ink.
